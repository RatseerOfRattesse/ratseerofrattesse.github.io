LORE ARTICLE // AURAL GEOMORPHOLOGY //

A nerd hyperfixates and rambles about magic's influence on the formation of terrain.

RUNES & AURA IN

geomorphology

An absolute idiot's understanding and fascination with auraflow and how it interacts with geomorphology to create the natural beauty of our planet. Waterfalls are fucking metal.

Aura plays just as big a role in the surface of Kaaldenvale as air, water, and animals. It's not just a force that we draw out of the ether to employ, it is so much more than that, and I wish more people knew it. It's moved and changed things this entire time, and geologists are only just beginnning to dig into how it's influenced our world without our hand.

So, I'm here to tell you all about the little bit of research I did and just how fucking cool it is.

I [ Auraflow & Erosion

So, I'm really no stranger to the hows and whys of auraflow. I'm an artificer! I make adventurer supplies for a living; imbuing such gear with special functionality requires essentially carving rituals into their fabrics and plates - and ritual patterns and runes are really just divots that guide aura to move and thus behave in specific ways. It's quite a relaxing pastime, drafting ritual patterns, once you kind of... well, basically, get to know aura. Intuition mostly.

But, how does this apply to aural geomorphology?1

II [ Examples in the Backyard//

Evacuations are being organized but the Diorvana dasannak's dimensional membrane is refusing to admit anything but metaphysical matter. Daethaian analysts have arrived and are studying the phenomenon with no estimate of an unlock time. Communications with the rest of the dasannak's planets are still up and the local proxy government will soon be unable to reign in the populus.

Loss of control over the warpways is estimated within six to ten rotations; even if the Daethai are able to unlock the membrane or the locals stop the effect, Diorvanan society will need reformations. Their position as a major manufacturing hub - especially Neverglades - will have lasting impact to the seventh dasanqi's economy. All investigative effort into the silenced planets have been given up and the populus of all the dasannak are migrating as fast as possible away from the Neverglades.

III [ Wild Rituals and Rune Diagrams//

The neighbouring planets Gardinal, Etshini, and Corrundal each sent fleets of two to six daysahnsi class battleships and a collective two hundred seventy smaller combat ships to land at multiple of Neverglade's sahnadocks, equipped with the region's top of the line technology. These ships arrived on the planet surface without issue and passed many Neverglade ships cut along perfect seams, at a fidelity and precision suggesting a mass attack by metaweaponry yet to be industrialized in the Daethaian territories, much less the wider cosmos.

Expeditions arrived at three separate sahnadocks at equidistant points around the planet, each inactive. Buildings showed more of the aforementioned perfect seams. Without the velocity of the cut ships in orbit, the seams were thin enough that almost every structure not impacted by a fallen ship was still upright and left no debris. There were no bodies.

IV [ Practical Applications//

The recovered expedition data has been sent off to the Church of Five Deities and the Daethai labs. It is undergoing analysis and the Interdasannal Body will be updated as according to progress.

There is no solid hypothesis on the phenomenon currently happening in Diorvana. The similarity of the 90-degree godspeak to the runes read from the recent extradahnal intrusion is noted. The runes and arbitrarily placed cuts also point to this being an incident caused by sentients (whether from malice or otherwise) rather than some natural phenomena. However, natural disaster is not to be placed out of the picture entirely during investigation; runes, especially godspeak, do appear naturally as a result of extreme metaphysical forces.
