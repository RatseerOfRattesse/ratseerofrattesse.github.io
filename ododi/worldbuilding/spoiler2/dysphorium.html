LORE ARTICLE // MASS DYSPHORIA ATTACKS //

Mysterious monsters silence entire planets in days, multiversal community concerned.

C.S.D. INTERDASANNAL COUNCIL // EVENT REPORT // 15 SMR. C1212 // 27:011

LOSS OF COMMUNICATIONS WITH DIORVANA

WRITTEN BY SEER KARASTA SABASTI C.E. // LOC. NEVERGLADE (PL-76) // DIORVANA DASANNAK

FOR THE EYES OF THE COUNCIL ONLY:

I | REPORT//

On the fifteenth of Samiron, Cycle 1212, communicators encountered a LOSS OF SIGNAL on all Neverglade feeds and channels across the Six Majors. Subsequent attempts to contact Neverglade were unsuccessful. It should be noted that this silencing was not gradual; the entirety of the Neverglade surface, orbit, and several ships moving to and from its orbit, were all cut off at the moment. Estimating from the average reaction time of interdasannakal channel operators, the process could have taken no less than 3.5 ahnsecs.

Neighbouring Diorvanan systems were successfully contacted and were no less able to reach Neverglade. After a rotation, these star systems were likewise silenced, and then their neighbours, and the process is still continuing through the Diorvana dasannak. A few expeditions were sent to the Neverglade surface and of other silenced worlds; none returned. Some data was recovered and transmitted out of the dasannak - see section III.

Immediately after the event, the auraspherical feeds of the planet changed; see figures one and two. Guides are currently inquiring on the meaning of what we believe is godspeak.

II | STATUS//

Evacuations are being organized but the Diorvana dasannak's dimensional membrane is refusing to admit anything but metaphysical matter. Daethaian analysts have arrived and are studying the phenomenon with no estimate of an unlock time. Communications with the rest of the dasannak's planets are still up and the local proxy government will soon be unable to reign in the populus.

Loss of control over the warpways is estimated within six to ten rotations; even if the Daethai are able to unlock the membrane or the locals stop the effect, Diorvanan society will need reformations. Their position as a major manufacturing hub - especially Neverglades - will have lasting impact to the seventh dasanqi's economy. All investigative effort into the silenced planets have been given up and the populus of all the dasannak are migrating as fast as possible away from the Neverglades.

III | EXPEDITION DATA//

The neighbouring planets Gardinal, Etshini, and Corrundal each sent fleets of two to six daysahnsi class battleships and a collective two hundred seventy smaller combat ships to land at multiple of Neverglade's sahnadocks, equipped with the region's top of the line technology. These ships arrived on the planet surface without issue and passed many Neverglade ships cut along perfect seams, at a fidelity and precision suggesting a mass attack by metaweaponry yet to be industrialized in the Daethaian territories, much less the wider cosmos.

Expeditions arrived at three separate sahnadocks at equidistant points around the planet, each inactive. Buildings showed more of the aforementioned perfect seams. Without the velocity of the cut ships in orbit, the seams were thin enough that almost every structure not impacted by a fallen ship was still upright and left no debris. There were no bodies.

Across any wall or other surface perched at a ninety-degree angle, the godspeak 'di deiehn sisahdfas' was painted in multicolour fluids of unknown composition, now dried.

On touching the runes, each fleet lost signal after about 0.25 ahnsecs, during which live feeds transmitted the images in figure two. Contact has yet to be reestablished and unlikely to be; a satellite promptly inserted into Neverglade orbit found the sites of all three visited sahnadocks with new perfectly cut wreckages.

IV | ANALYSIS//

The recovered expedition data has been sent off to the Church of Six Deities and the Daethai labs. It is undergoing analysis and the Interdasannal Council will be updated as according to progress.

There is no solid hypothesis on the phenomenon currently happening in Diorvana. Speculations guess it may relate to the Chaos' recent descension, if only for lack of other large scale interdasannal events in the past cyclade. The similarity of the 90-degree godspeak to the runes read from the recent extradahnal intrusion is noted. The runes and arbitrarily placed cuts also point to this being an incident caused by sentients (whether from malice or otherwise) rather than some natural phenomena. However, natural disaster is not to be placed out of the picture entirely during investigation; runes, especially godspeak, do appear naturally as a result of extreme metaphysical forces.

This incident has been moved to the top of the CSD jobstack. The power of whatever force is overtaking Diorvana, whether a natural phenomena or otherwise, has demonstrated itself capable of taking down not just one but tens of some of our most powerful planets in a single rotation. The ring of expansion is currently predicted to consume thirty-eight planets by next rotation.

The lifeforms or otherwise displayed in fig. 2 of the above section are unknown and do not match any biological records kept at the Interdasannal Library. Analysts are currently making their way through the CSD archives looking for them. Whether they are the direct cause of the incident or an accompanying symptom is unknown.
