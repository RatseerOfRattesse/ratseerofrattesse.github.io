01 - PITCH || WELCOME, VIEWER //

our deities of dreadful ineptitude

is a WORK IN PROGRESS high fantasy webcomic about adventurer Aryon Hastor, who may or may not be getting hunted by an interdimensional rat invasion for her jewelry. She's doing just great.

JUST GREAT

THE CAST

Along for the ride is her sister Bia, the gang's makeshift therapist and fiction enthusiast, Lloyd the idealistic copium snorter with no strings attached, Arodorros the mysterious mentor, and friendly neighbourhood goofballs Rosa and Dan.

As the only adventurers resident in low level Javenshard, Ari and co. have a responsibility to defend the town from monsters. It's a simple life, but Ari makes expendable income and that's enough. So, when an extradimensional manifestation of destruction shows up in their forest next to the level one rats, it's a... bit of a wake-up call.

RESPONSIBILITY?

ADVENTURE !

Of course, they can't just let the thing run free - nobody can stop it. It's only right for them to report it to the guilds, but Ari doesn't feel like trekking the dangerous path to the capital...
