Crazy shit going down in Vaerys rn

> RORI is talking

pictured: the usual theologic graphic

What's up my prankster gangsters?!! Rori's here for another bite'a the 'ol information dispensation. There's been a lotta ruckus coming out of the local C.S.D. cathedral as of late. People getting fired, people getting hired (lav's lost one of their five million different side hustles), lots of yelling. The divine light has been blasting out the windows more often than it should. I'm gonna need thicker curtains! I live right by one of the cardinal streets to the divine square. I'd complain about lost sleep but I don't sleep and also I get to see cool shit. Like, one of the demidaesas came by yesterday. Flew straight through the street and the cathedral doors, got everyone looking out their windows. And like they were kinda pretty??? But yeah it's been exciting these past few...

All anyone knows isat the clergy are really high strung right now. Assemblies are just straight up and entirely paused right now - I know plenty of folk who aren't happy about that. God fearing citizens feeling entitled to godly traditions. Everyone's in uproar. I heard some of Lais' followers have gone vegetative - think I might have seen one near Oisne. He was all drabbed up in clergyrobes and just staring at the sky. I don't know much of the holy dress so I can't be sure that was a Laisa but I bet.

I don't know many Laisa folk myself. Lais always seemed to be the right pick though. The others are so high strung - well I suppose Zezakand is a little more chill with his media appearances and all but the guy is literally death. The daddy death dasanet trend is not okay and he needs to stop enabling it. Um but yeah Lais just seems more fun than the rest. Shame about what's going on with her people.

I heard this kind of stuff is going all around the dasanet. Even the Amirlith C.S.D. branch is supposedly going wild. They must have it terrible over there, everything goes through the C.S.D. right? I never got how people survived Amirlith life, so much ceremony and all. I guess it's mostly full of the higher power people who have already dealt with that kinda stuff. Though, I do recall them having plentiful moksha supply. I would love to have regular meals with mokshabloom, I'd run through any amount of C.S.D. hoops.

Lav is nervous. They think something really bad is happening and we should lay low. Stay inside and keep off the dasanet, especially about these events. On the first count, probably fine. Look at what I'm writing to you on, you think I see the suns? Second - well Lav can't blame me for mouthing what everyone else is. If there is some crackdown on talking about this stuff I'm sure they can't get all of us. And like, there's nobody else reading this blog anyway.

But I get them. It is kind of worrying, some of the rumours going around. They're saying all sorts of stuff. The daesas are punishing the sinners. The daesas are instating some new law, or uprooting Amirlith, or whatever all sorts. I heard a few of the clergyfolk wanted out, there was a mass exodus. They figured out the old precursor ruins over in Actinidei, found something horrible. Someone got cast from the pantheon, or left, or something. The apocalypse is coming. I'm not worried but I'm worried that Lav is.

I'm gonna sign off here. Will definitely right more is more stuff happens. Exciting times aye?! If that doomsday's coming or whatever it is, the world will have a record hosted off a corner of my house. I'm officially an MVP now. Here's to preserving history!

Love,

Big Boss Rori :3
