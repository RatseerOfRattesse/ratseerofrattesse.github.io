01 - HALOS WHO?

about me <3

Hi there! I'm Ratseer(OfRattesse), I art, and I may or may not work for the rat shadow government. I also dip my toes in fields of writing and programming, so that I may suffer from my creative endeavours thricefold and occasionally shit out something halfway worth the trouble.

I make stories and the occasional game/coded project. My prize jewel is, of course, the illustriously incompletable ODODI. Fun acronym, no? It used to be called CoA. Oh, the horror. An-yway, ODODI is a very cool book webcomic that is currently IN PLANNING. You can read that original incomplete book on RoyalRoad.

It's about this kinda piece of shit girlfailure adventurer Ari who happens to be getting hunted by an interdimensional rat invasion + the humanoid embodiment of death and edginess. She's doing great. Along for the ride is her sister Bia, the gang's makeshift therapist and fiction enthusiast, Lloyd the idealistic copium snorter with no strings attached, Arodorros the mysterious mentor, and friendly neighbourhood goofballs Rosa and Dan.

03 - WORLD DOMINATION

The other thing I am currently working on is Celestial Capitalist. Also found on idi0cy's Stardace and my Stardance. It's a... sort of idle? game where you start as a homeless person on the streets and eventually grow to world domination corporatocracy through a lot of stupid minigames and other stuff we thought would be cool to drop in there. It's the brain child of my friend idi0cy, who does most of the programming while I do the art, some code, and making up dumbass ideas like union buster tower defence. It's a WORK IN PROGRESS but NEARING INITIAL RELEASE.

Another current project is NarraStack, a comprehensive writer/worldbuilder's editor for me to survive having a million ideas for ODODI at once.

I'm a massive fan of a ton of different stories/media. Among the top are my favourite webcomics - the heartwarming Kochab, the sick as hell Goodbye to Halos, the whimsical ON A SUNBEAM, and the what the fuck is even happening anymore Countdown to Countdown, quickly followed by the excellently witty and badass He Who Fights With Monsters, the Freeze Frame Revolution (and Mr. Watts' other horrors), Drehmal, the worlds of Simon Stalenhag, Project Hail Mary (I read the book before the movie came out!!!!!), and the works of David Armsby. There is also, of course, the cringily incredible favourites Songs of War and Liam Vickers' many entirely finished endeavours, both of which will always have an integral hand in my art and place in my heart no matter how many times we go through the Mary Sue motions.

Also, Necry Talkie is the best fucking band ever and I have yet to consume any other Japanese media. Taida de Iina lives rent free in my head and Oshare Daisakusen is ready to strike me dead at 25.

05 - SIDE QUESTS

Some other projects I have going are Laissez Faire, a Minecraft overhaul aimed towards builders to remove tedious grinding from the build creation cycle and utilizing villagers more, in SEMI-ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT.

I've also got another comic lying around that has been PUSHED TO THE WAYSIDE but still exists. It's called the Thousand Brushstroke Sun and is about these two hopeless romantics Bismuth and Ametrine who may or may not cause the end of the world and three-body singer the last civilization in the universe. It's fine, they didn't mean it.

There's also a bajillion more MC mods, such as Halos Cosmetics, a custom clothing platform for me to make a bunch of fancy outifts for my roleplay, Create: Compound Armour, a solution for creating armoured vehicles in Create Aeronautics. It generates a bunch of chemical formulas based on the world seed that allow you to make customizable high strength blocks in a mimicry of a weapons race. There's also Realmbinder, another MC mod based on the magic system of Drehmal, all of which are STAGNATING in the 'ill do it when i want' list.

Andddd I have a game called Wagonload on the FAR BACKBURNER that's gonna have some crazy voxel world generation and RTS features. That's so getting done.
