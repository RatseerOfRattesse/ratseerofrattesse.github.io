The Thousand Brushstroke Sun is currently as-concept webcomic about these two hopeless romantics Bismuth and Ametrine who may or may not cause the end of the world and abstractify the last civilization in the universe and everybody in it. It's fine, they didn't mean it.

This idea came to be after I made the decision to stop writing ODODI as a novel and instead turn to webcomics. I had thought, that since I wanted ODODI to be perfect, I should make a short proper comic first to get my grip on the medium. I came up with this concept out of my love for art and also because I wanted to draw yuri. Anyway, I then got way too excited about ODODI again as I was writing more plot for it and ended up putting TBS ON HOLD to just jump straight into ODODI as a comic. I even added yuri to the second volume.

Currently just a CONCEPT. I have many ideas, a plot outline, some concept art, and I think it's gonna be a good story.

DETAILS ->

Narrastack allows you to stack narratives - not in that fancy metaphysical way but in the way of actually allowing your brain to function with five hundred different worldbuilding and character and plot elements all stacked on top of each other. It's a headache all the time, but Narrastack's here to lessen that burden.

It's a site builder and local worldbuilding tool. If you want, you can keep everything inside Narrastack to plan internally. Or, if you want to develop a wiki for the web, you can do so with ease. Render with your site's CSS, and give the export function instructions so that you can export an HTML that wires into your site effortlessly.

Well, that's the concept anyway.

While plotting ODODI, I found that juggling all the variables in my head was a pain in the ass. I found that drafting things onto timelines was really helping out, but paper is too limiting, so I came up with this idea. It'll happen... sometime.

DETAILS ->

Laissez Faire is a Minecraft mod that aims to overhaul progression and balance to allow builders to spend their time building instead of grinding resources, and to overall make the game more fun. This is an opinionated overhaul. I do not presume to say that the game isn't fun and this fixes it; I say this helps me stay engaged with a world. LF does this by rebalancing villager trades for building and resource diversification, making structures provide more unique resources, and replacing farms by allowing you to run mines, plantations, etc. using villagers you have to pay.

Currently IN DEVELOPMENT! It was originally made for Fabric 26.2 serverside for a small MC server my friends and I wanted to run, but I quickly realized I did not like making datapacks and would like to return to the sacred clutches of the Java Virtual Machine. It's now being rebuilt for Neoforge 1.21.1, as that is the version I most personally play on.

Celestial Capitalist is a semi-idle game where the player simultaneously builds and ascends the corporate ladder from the bottom of the street to the self-built throne at the top of the cosmos. Beg your way from homelessness, run a lemonade stand, scam grandmas on the internet, start a cult, play union-buster tower defence, and generally just have a grand stupid time while the world slowly bends to your will.

Currently on FIRST RELEASE! Contains the slightly unpolished first stage, Rock Bottom, with basic progression, a bunch of minigames and... style!.

Project concept and lead by idi0cy.
